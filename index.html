Who We Are

About Jessie

AI Powered
ESP32-S3
Python Backend

Jessie is a voice-driven AI desk companion that sees, listens and watches over its surroundings. It keeps the user company, keeps them alert and safe, and sends emergency alerts to their phone, while heavy AI processing runs on a Python backend so the low-cost ESP32-S3 stays fast and light.

🎙️ Voice Driven 👁️ Vision AI 🔔 Emergency Alerts ⚡ Lightweight
What Jessie Can Do

Core Features

Eight intelligent capabilities built into one compact companion.

XiaoZhi AI Features

Powered by XiaoZhi's conversational AI engine for natural, real-time voice interaction and intelligent responses.

Smoke & Fire Detection

Continuously monitors the environment for signs of smoke or fire and instantly sends alerts to keep you safe.

Sleep & Drowsiness Detection

Detects when you doze off or become drowsy and gently alerts you to stay awake and focused.

Sound Detection

Listens for unusual sounds, loud noises, or specific audio cues and responds with appropriate notifications.

Owner Recognition

Learns and identifies its owner's face and voice to deliver personalized responses and enhanced security.

Bad Posture Detection

Monitors your sitting posture using computer vision and reminds you to sit up straight before pain sets in.

Mood Finder

Analyses facial expressions in real-time to gauge your mood and adapt its tone, suggestions, and interactions accordingly.

Reminders

Proactively reminds you of tasks, hydration, breaks, and scheduled events using voice and visual cues.
